RevoyLofiCyberpunk.png and RevoyLofiCyberpunk-640.png are crops of
"2021-09-29 lofi cyberpunk" by David Revoy, licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2021-09-29_lofi_cyberpunk_by_david-revoy.jpg

Changes: cropped to (760, 228) - (1570, 876) of the 2431 x 1930 original and
resized with Lanczos to 160 x 128 (RevoyLofiCyberpunk.png, the resampler's
source) and 640 x 512 (RevoyLofiCyberpunk-640.png, what a 4x resample should
look like).  MakeRevoyLofiCyberpunk.py made them (needs Pillow):

    python3 MakeRevoyLofiCyberpunk.py 2021-09-29_lofi_cyberpunk_by_david-revoy.jpg .
