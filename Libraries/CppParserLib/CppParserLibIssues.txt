CppParserLib: issues left from the quality sweep
================================================

What is left of the sweep of CppParserLib for code smells, duplication and anything wrong
or inelegant (2026-11).  Items 1 to 96 and 98 to 100 are done (56 was not a copy: GuardedBy's
narrower walk is needed, and says why); the numbers are kept as they were.  Written 2026-11-07,
updated 2026-11-08.


Limits and findings
-------------------

101. class X; and enum X; leave one node out of the tree: the specifier made before it's known to be elaborated.
102. An error in an uncalled template instance doesn't fail CCPPSemanticPass::Check, and #define with nothing after it
     is reported with no position.
103. Rvalue references (B10 in CppParserTodo.txt) aren't a type of their own: && is made a & (GetDeclaratorType).
     Rechecked 2026-11-08 on the 65C64.  E14 only made T&& and Args&& pass arguments on (ForwardedType).  Works:
     passing through T&&, and a temporary by value.  Wrong with no error: int&& binds a variable; F(const int&) and
     F(int&&) are one function, so F(3) calls the first; T&& never deduces T as int&, so T u = t copies; && and &
     ref-qualified members are the same (S().F() calls the & one).  Errors: int&& taking a temporary; F(int&) with
     F(int&&) ("defined twice"); int&& locals, returns and fields; (S&&) and static_cast<S&&>, so no move constructor or
     move assignment is chosen; R<T&&> as a specialisation; perfect forwarding and the shape of std::move; T&& for a
     value is const, so changing a copy of it is an error.  A pack that calls itself (S(A&&, B&&...)) doesn't find the
     template either.  There are no implicit move constructors or xvalues.
97. std:: and its library: a separate job, for the end.  The generator has what it needs of
    namespaces and argument-dependent lookup.
