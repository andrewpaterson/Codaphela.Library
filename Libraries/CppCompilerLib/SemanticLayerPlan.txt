CppCompilerLib: the semantic layer, put right
=============================================

the user's "Plan fixes for the semantic layer".  The findings say where each thing is and what the clean form
would be, and are not repeated here.  ConsolidationPlan.txt's stages 3 to 5 are where this layer got its shape;
this plan finishes what they left and what has been added since.

Where it is (2026-11-24): the first part's stages (0 to 16) are done, but for what each stage's record says it
left; stage 7 was tried and taken back; its last table is at the end.  Part two (stages 17 to 25, planned

questions 11 and 12 were asked and answered as assumed.  Stages 17 to 23 are done (2026-11-24, their
records at the end; 23e's place question was tried and taken back); 24 is next.  The user on 2026-11-22: "Finish and commit,
continue with all fixes in the semantic layer as planned.  Commit and clean-up as you go along."

The rules (the questions say which the user settled, 2026-11-22)

  1. The code the compiler writes stays as it is in every stage but two.  This layer decides what the C++ means;
     putting its decisions in one place should not change them.  A stage marked "same code" must leave every
     unit's listing as it was.  Stages 8 and 11 may change it (which declarations are one function; which
     constants are folded): there ConsolidationPlan's rule holds (it must work, and ideally is faster), with
     OptimiserPlan's bar: every program of the three *65 suites passes on both processors and no suite's total
     of cycles or of bytes is worse on either.
  2. What the compiler says stays as it is too, but where saying it twice, or without a place, was the fault
     (question 3).  Every difference in a diagnostic is read and goes in the record.
  3. A class taken out of the unit is given what it needs when it is made (the types, the context, the
     diagnostics, the tokeniser) and does not hold the unit if that is enough.  Where it must still ask the
     unit, what it asks is counted and listed in the record: finding 44 says the classes that left before ask
     it nearly everything, and this plan must not add to that.
  4. CPP65 becomes CPP in a name when its class or table is reworked here (ConsolidationPlan's decision):
     CCPP65CheckChildren, CCPP65CheckLambdas, gasCPP65Builtins.  SCPP65Type and CCPP65Types are the type
     checker's area's and keep their names.
  5. A helper that replaces several copies gets cases of its own in TestCppCompilerLib.
  6. The work is done in the CommonLibraries checkout itself (D:\gameengine on Windows, Codaphela on Linux, where
     stages 17 on were done), on master, not in a worktree (the user,
     2026-11-22: "Why do you keep working in worktrees?").  A stage is committed when it has passed, by its own
     paths.  Nothing is pushed.  It is no longer the only work in the compiler (2026-11-24): the debugger's
     (DebuggerPlan.txt; its compiler stages A1 to A4 and C1 to C3 are in, F1 to F4 would touch the compiler
     again) and HashMapPlan.txt's conversions land between stages.  So, as ConsolidationPlan's "The plan from
     here" does: a stage is small enough to make, prove and commit in one sitting, nothing is left staged
     between stages, and the listings' baseline is taken again, with the reason written in the record, when
     another commit moves them (question 11).
  7. Plan first; measure before and after.
