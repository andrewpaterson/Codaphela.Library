CppCompilerLib: the semantic layer, put right
=============================================

A plan for findings 15 to 29 of SmellCheckFindings.txt: CCPPUnit and the classes round it.  Written 2026-11-22 at
the user's "Plan fixes for the semantic layer".  The findings say where each thing is and what the clean form
would be, and are not repeated here.  ConsolidationPlan.txt's stages 3 to 5 are where this layer got its shape;
this plan finishes what they left and what has been added since.

Where it is: stage 0 is begun (2026-11-22).  The user answered questions 1, 2, 3 and 5 that day; question 4
is still open, and nothing before stage 15 depends on it.


What was checked before writing (2026-11-22, master 9a11fabbd)

  - The findings were read at about 95556e055; 51 commits have changed the compiler since.  Every name the fifteen
    findings rest on was looked for again: all of it is still there (the four dead members of 29 still have no
    caller, CCPPWarnings, IsComparison and IsAssignment still don't exist, mcData.GetText() is still reached 93
    times from the unit, 70 of them in CPPUnitVtables.cpp).  Line numbers have moved; the names have not.
  - CCPPUnit has 605 functions in 13 files and 22,117 lines today.  It had 219 at stage 5's checkpoint

  - 40 of the diagnostics no test raised (appendix A, at b87a252bc) are in the CPPUnit*.cpp files, and 6 in
    CPPConstants.cpp.


The rules (the questions say which the user settled, 2026-11-22)

  1. The code the compiler writes stays as it is in every stage but two.  This layer decides what the C++ means;
     putting its decisions in one place should not change them.  A stage marked "same code" must leave every
     unit's listing as it was.  Stages 8 and 11 may change it (which declarations are one function; which
     constants are folded): there ConsolidationPlan's rule holds (it must work, and ideally is faster), with
     OptimiserPlan's bar: every program of the three *65 suites passes on both processors and no suite's total
     of cycles or of bytes is worse on either.
  2. What the compiler says stays as it is too, but where saying it twice, or without a place, was the fault
     (question 3).  Every difference in a diagnostic is read and goes in the record.
  3. A class taken out of the unit is given what it needs when it is made (the types, the context, the
     diagnostics, the tokeniser) and does not hold the unit if that is enough.  Where it must still ask the
     unit, what it asks is counted and listed in the record: finding 44 says the classes that left before ask
     it nearly everything, and this plan must not add to that.
  4. CPP65 becomes CPP in a name when its class or table is reworked here (ConsolidationPlan's decision):
     CCPP65CheckChildren, CCPP65CheckLambdas, gasCPP65Builtins.  SCPP65Type and CCPP65Types are the type
     checker's area's and keep their names.
  5. A helper that replaces several copies gets cases of its own in TestCppCompilerLib.
  6. A stage is merged to master when it has passed.  The work is done in D:\gameengine-semantic (branch
     semantic-layer), brought up to master before each stage.  Nothing is pushed.
  7. Plan first; measure before and after.


How a stage is proved

  - TestCppCompilerLib whole (RunParallel.py, about 165 s).
  - "Same code": each of the three *65 suites' projects compiled for each processor a unit at a time with the
    listings kept (TEST_PROJECT65_LISTINGS, TEST_PROJECT65_ONLY=NoSuchTest, TEST_PROJECT65_SERIAL=1), and every
    listing compared with the stage before's, from the same folder.
  - Otherwise (stages 8 and 11): the three suites run on both processors (Tests/CompilerMeasure.py --raw), every
    program's cycles and bytes set beside the stage before's.
  - The same diagnostics: stage 0 makes the kit.  Every conformance case's diagnostics (file, line, column,
    text), from a build on each target and from the semantic pass, written to one file and compared with the
    stage before's.  TestCppErrors and the conformance lists pin texts already; this pins all of them.
  - The IDE: HCP65-IDE builds, and its checks pass as they did (the known failures stay the known failures),
    at stages 4, 7, 10 and 14 and at the end: those are the ones that touch what the semantic pass reports.
  - The 20 HCP65 projects and TestLibc65 at stages 4, 8, 11 and 15 and at the end.
  - The compiler's own time and counts for TestSupportLib65's project (CompilerMeasure.py --compile --serial):
    the declarations, types and front end phases, TypeOf's calls and those worked out, quiet trials and those
    that failed, and stage 0's new counts.  TestCPP65Declarations' asserted counts stay as they are.  A stage
    that makes the compiler slower says so and why.
  - The machine has 7.9 GB: a *65 suite and TestCppCompilerLib are never run at the same time.


The stages

Each names its findings and its size (S, M, L, XL as ConsolidationPlan's).  The order is by what needs what:
the tests that are missing; what only takes away; then the two things every class taken out must be handed
(where the compiler is, and where its errors go); then each idea that is written several times, made one;
then the jobs that are left, which by then need little from the unit.  A job leaves the unit in the stage
that makes it one thing, so stage 15 has only what no earlier stage took.

0. The baseline, the diagnostics kit, and what reading suspected, run.  (M)

   - The measures above, taken at the plan's commit and kept (the record).
   - The diagnostics kit: a switch of TestCPPConformance that writes what each case made the compiler say.
   - Counts for what this plan removes, so each stage can show they went: errors made and then taken away
     (Truncate), work run a second time only to report (ReportTypeOf, ReportFailedDeclaration, the second
     DeclareFunction), constants asked of each of the three evaluators, base lists walked, and mpcUnit-> in
     each class beside the unit.
   - The findings say six things "may" go wrong and ran none of them.  Each gets a program, tried on both
     targets and by g++:
       16  a type remembered by TypeOf inside sizeof or decltype and asked for again outside it (the memo's
           key has no "unevaluated");
       16  this, after a function scope is entered and left inside a member function (LeaveFunctionScope
           works mbThis out again), and after NoThis, which nothing leaves;
       17  MayThrow's early return, which skips its Truncate;
       20  a pack's binding left holding element k when the pattern fails between the set and the clear;
       23  two declarations that differ only in a const below the top level, at the two places that compare
           with Equals and not Same (a generic lambda's instance; CCPPOverloads' two loops);
       25  a variable marked used only because its label was written (AppendVariableLabel).
     What is wrong becomes a conformance case and is put right in the stage that owns it (or here, if it is a
     line).  What is right is said to be so, with the reason.
   - A case, with its text asserted, for each of the 46 diagnostics of appendix A in this layer that still has
     none.  Stage 4 changes how every one of them is reported, so they are pinned first.  One that no source
     can raise is said to be so, and why.

1. What is dead, and what says what is not so (29).  Same code.  (S)

   IsNullPointerLiteral, CCPPVtable::IsOverriddenBy, CCPPContext::IsStaticLocal and mbStaticLocal go;
   EndFunction loses its parameter.  The stale comments (CPPUnitOverloads.cpp's and CPPUnitLabels.cpp's
   headers, AddFunction's overload number, ExpandPackCall, the 16 "the generator"s) say what is true.  The two
   files are named for what they hold, or their functions go where their jobs go in later stages (said then).

2. The small questions (26).  Same code.  (M)

   - IsComparison and IsAssignment are a token's (columns of the punctuator table), and every list of the six
     comparison operators or of the assignment operators in the library reads them: the unit's five, and the
     type checker's and the IR front end's too, because one helper with two lists left beside it is not done.
     That closes those lists of findings 39 and 52 (question 2).
   - What an integer type can hold is a range from the type (least, most), asked by the four places that work
     it out, ConstantFits, ConstantOfType and IntegerLiteralType.
   - Names the compiler knows (__PRETTY_FUNCTION__, memcpy, the float builtins) are interned once, as
     mlliOverride is; no hand-counted length is left.
   - CCPPSymbol::DeclSpecifiers() for "the specifiers this was declared with" (nine walks);
     SCPP65Type::ElementType(); WithoutReference() where it is written out (twelve).

3. Where the compiler is (16).  Same code.  (M)

   - The function is entered and left like the rest of the context: an entry that keeps the function, whether
     there is a this, its locals and its packs.  EnterFunctionScope's memcpy goes; mbThis is restored, not
     worked out again.  Who enters a function leaves it: the unit, on success too (the IR front end's half is
     finding 46's; it is done here because one cannot be right without the other).
   - NoThis, quiet and unevaluated are entries, so CheckContext catches one left unbalanced.  "Unevaluated"
     has one answer; the walk up the tree goes.  If stage 0's program shows TypeOf's memo can be wrong, the
     key gets it; if it cannot, the record says why.
   - mpcDefining is GlobalDeclaration's parameter.  mbValueArgumentRefused is what ValueArgumentFits and
     FoldConstant return.
   - One guard for "this is being worked out now" (mapcObjectConstants, mapcConstantTypes, CCPPConstants'
     mapcWorking, miWarningChecks), entered and left through the context.
   - The element of a pack being expanded is a context entry (stage 5 uses it).
   - CPPContext.h's "nothing is saved and put back by hand" is true.  TestCPP65Context grows to each entry.
   Ends when: no member of the unit is set before a call and put back after it.

4. Errors (17, and 36).  (L)

   - CCPPDiagnostics: the errors, the warnings and the trials (job a of finding 15, about 20 functions).  One
     body for an error and a warning at a node or a token; the eight ways in become what they really are:
     an error here, and an error kept with a result.
   - A trial owns its errors.  What is tried writes to the trial's list, loud or not, and the caller keeps
     them or drops them.  The 25 calls of Truncate go (14 in the unit today; 6 in CCPPBinder, 2 in
     CCPPOverloads, 2 in the IR front end and one each in the type checker and CCPPConstants: finding 36 is
     this same mechanism seen from the binder, and two mechanisms cannot share one list, so they go
     together).  With them go 36's other three: "did the callee say something" by counting errors,
     CCPPAccess::Refuse (which is ReportOnce written again), and the bReport parameters that run a binding a
     second time to say its errors.
   - A remembered result keeps its errors.  TypeOf's memo, a class's layout, a closure, a declaration that
     failed and a constant each hold what was said when they were worked out, and reporting is appending that
     list: ReportTypeOf and ReportFailedDeclaration stop running the work again, the second DeclareFunction
     for operator new and delete goes, and so do masClassErrors, SCPPLambda's szError and pcErrorNode,
     mapcFailed's re-run and ReportOnce's strstr over every error.
   - Quiet mode is left for what only asks "does this work": ConsolidationPlan's stage 4 step 9 sorted the
     sites once; they are sorted again, and each is a trial (its errors may be wanted) or a question.
   - The errors with no place (ErrorAt(-1, ...): the finding names three, there are seven today) get the node
     that is at hand, where one is.
   Not same diagnostics, on purpose: an error said twice is said once, and some gain a place.  Nothing else
   may differ.  Stage 0's counts of errors taken away and of work run again are zero at the end.

5. One way through a pack (20).  Same code.  (M)

   One "for each element of these packs" (it finds the packs a pattern names, checks their lengths, enters each
   element through stage 3's entry), used by ExpandPattern, ExpandTemplateArguments and DeclareSignature.  The
   two messages are said in one place; SCPPExpandedArgument has an Init.  Nothing writes an element into the
   pack's own binding.  CCPPPacks holds it with the pack tables (masPackElements, mapapsPacks, mapalliPacks,
   mapcPackSymbols): CPPUnitPacks.cpp's 16 functions leave the unit.

6. A class's bases (21).  Same code.  (M)

   CCPPClass lists its base paths once, when its bases are resolved: each path's class, offset, whether it is
   public all the way and whether it goes through a virtual base.  IsDerived, CountBases, CountFixedBases,
   VirtualBaseOf, CompleteOffset, IsPublicBase, IsOnePublicBase (and CountBasePaths' nine parameters),
   FindInBases, IsAmbiguousInBases, HasMember's walk, FindBaseInstance, CCPPClass::HasBase and
   CCPPAccess::HasBase read the list.  The four answers to "is B a base of C" become one function whose
   parameters say what each caller means (itself counted or not), and each difference between today's four is
   looked at: meant, or an accident (then a case, and the stage is not same code for that caller).  One
   CollectVirtualBases, in the layout's order.  mpcBase goes.  Only ResolveBases stays with the unit;
   AllowsOnlyWhat moves beside CCPPExceptionSpec.

7. Which class a name means (22).  Same code.  (M-L)

   One resolver from a CCPPSyntaxName to what it names (a class, a type or a member, with the bindings of the
   class that declares it), the binder's.  The fifteen ways in become calls of it; the choice between
   GetComponentClass and QualifiersClass is made once, inside; GetComponentClass's ladder on the symbol's kind
   and CCPPDeclarators' twin of it are one.  Stage 5 of ConsolidationPlan bound names in expressions; this is
   the same for type names and qualifiers.  The IDE's GetResolved is checked after it.

8. A function's signature (23).  (M)

   - CCPPSignature, made once from a declarator: the kind, the name, the parameter types and defaults, the
     qualifiers and the flags.  DeclareFunction takes it in place of seven parameters; CCPPFunction can match
     one and adopt one.  The "(void)" rule and the parameter types from a suffix are written once.
   - One SameParameters, used by all six loops.  Four compare with Same and two with Equals today: stage 0
     says whether that is a wrong answer.  If it is, this stage changes what is one function, and is measured.
   - DeclareQuietly (with stage 4's trial: the declarator that failed keeps its errors) for the five copies;
     AddImplicitFunction for the eight; SCPPField::Init for the four.

9. Definitions outside their class template (24).  Same code.  (M)

   A definition written outside is classified once into a record: what it is (the fourteen arms and
   IsOutsideMemberTemplate's fifteenth become one function that returns a kind), the template that owns it, its
   name, its declaration.  One typed list of the records replaces the five untyped ones
   (mapcMemberSpecialisations, mapcMemberTemplateSpecialisations, mapcMemberClassTemplates,
   mapcMemberVariableTemplates, mapcStaticMemberTemplates), and no reader works the name out again through the
   cast chain.  Declarations walks the list once.

10. One printer of types (19).  Same code.  (S-M)

   CCPPTypeWriter with its policies as switches (may fail or not; "(void)"; placeholders; "<lambda>" and
   "(anonymous namespace)::").  AppendTypeName, AppendTypeNameAround, AppendClassName, AppendScopeName and
   AppendIdentifier go from the unit.  What type_info::name gives, what an error says of a type and what the
   IDE shows are each asserted before and after: the text may not move.

11. One evaluator of constants (18).  (L)

   - A constant is one value: an integer, a floating point number or an object, with its type.
   - CCPPConstantFolder, out of the unit (job b, about 30 functions and CPPUnitFloat.cpp): one walk of the
     tree for integers and floats together, so a named cast and ?: are folded for both.  It answers folded,
     not a constant, or "needs running", and CCPPConstants (the IR interpreter, which is clean and stays) is
     asked in that last case: RunConstant's third ladder goes.
   - "Is this name a const or constexpr variable with an initialiser" is asked in one place; signed overflow
     reads the operators' opcodes from the table the front end uses.
   - Stage 3's guard and stage 4's kept errors are what it stands on: mszReason is the constant's own errors.
   Not same code: a float constant the tree folder could not fold is initialised at run time today (finding 92
   saw it give the right answer) and is a constant in the data after.  Measured by the suites.  Every case of
   TestCPPConformance's constant-expression clauses is the proof that no constant changed its value.

12. Lambdas (27).  Same code.  (M)

   CCPPLambda with its own functions (the 21 that take SCPPLambda* first, of CPPUnitLambdas.cpp's 38).  One
   "finish laying out a class" (the implicit destructor, the vtable, the layout, the states) for LayoutClass
   and LayoutClosure.  A generic lambda's instance is found by stage 8's SameParameters.  A closure's errors
   are stage 4's.

13. Class data, labels and what runs at exit (25).  Same code.  (M)

   - CCPPClassData beside CCPPUnitData writes vtables, VTTs, type info and typeid names (CPPUnitVtables.cpp's
     18 functions and the type info part of CPPUnitObjects.cpp), through AppendData, AppendAddress and
     AppendSection: the unit and it never reach into the data's text.
   - CCPPMangler makes every label: static locals' S and G, __vcall_, the type info's, __Name, __Bound,
     R_Finalise and __ExitNode.  The IR front end asks it for the two it types out today.  The exit node's
     size and alignment are named once.
   - One record for each exit finaliser (label, type, shared) and for each thrown object's destroyer.
   - GlobalStartUpRoutines is two calls of one function, not a loop of two with six tests of which.
     BoundTemporary returns one thing.  AppendVariableLabel only appends; what must be marked used is marked
     by who uses it (stage 0 says whether today's side effect hides anything).
   - The eight functions that decode strings go to a class that needs only the tokeniser and the types.

14. The IDE's check asks; the compiler doesn't know it is asked (28).  Same code.  (S-M)

   EnumType makes an enum's type and knows nothing of the refactoring: the pass is told "a type was made"
   through the one interface the unit already tells it things by (Typed, Changed, Resolved), and marks the enum
   itself.  The null tests of mpcSemanticPass (about ten) become calls on an observer that does nothing when
   code is generated.  The check's own walk (CPPUnitCheck.cpp, 38 functions) becomes CCPPCheck, driven by the
   pass.  Finding 106 (Convert Enum's doubled cast) is another session's and is not touched: what the records
   hold stays as it is.

15. What is left of the dozen jobs (15).  Same code.  (XL, six parts, each proved and merged alone)

   By now the diagnostics (a), constants (b), lambdas (f), class data (g), names (h), bases (i), start-up and
   exit (j) and packs have gone in their own stages.  What is left:
     a. CCPPWarnings (c): the twelve checks of CPPUnitExpressions.cpp.  ConsolidationPlan's layer list has
        named it since 2026-11-11.
     b. CCPPBuiltins (k): one table for the builtins, the float builtins and the memcpy names, and the seven
        questions asked of it.
     c. Exception specifications (d), about 30, beside CCPPExceptionSpec: what a function is declared to
        throw, what an expression may throw, the pointer and assignment checks, CheckThrown and CheckTryJumps.
        Moved, not changed: the Exceptions group passes on both targets before and after.
     d. The desugarings (l): range-for, user-defined literals, braced lists regrouped, decltype members and
        the implicit arguments, each a small class over one "made once for a node" memo.
     e. What an expression is (m), about 50: value, place or object and what it binds to go to the binder;
        the conditional's and the composite pointer's types to the types; TypeOf and its memo to the type
        checker.  Moved as they are: bound nodes for the rest of an expression (ConsolidationPlan's stage 6)
        were dropped at the checkpoint and are not reopened here (question 4).
     f. Templates (e), CPPUnitTemplates.cpp's 75 functions less what stage 9 took: CCPPInstantiator makes a
        class's, a function's and a variable template's instance and gives the specialisations and outside
        definitions to them.
   Ends when: CCPPUnit is the walk of the declarations, the queue of functions and the unit's options, and
   owns the classes that do the rest.  The number it ends at is set at this stage's start, when what is left
   has been read; finding 15 puts the walk and the queue at about 60 functions.
