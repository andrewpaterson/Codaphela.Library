Scene plan
==========

CScene, CInstance, CConnection, CConnectionAnimation, CCamera and CLight describe a scene, in floats.  They aren't used yet,
and CScene still rests on CFakeTracker and TrackerTypes.  RasterLib draws, in integers.  The split is the same as CMesh and
CRasterMesh: one way, from the description to what is drawn.


The rule
--------
A step builds RasterLib's types from the scene.  RasterLib never includes the scene's headers.

These stay out of RasterLib:
  Transforms with parents        CConnection's local and world matrices.
  Lights as objects              CLight.  The rasteriser is given plain values to shade with.
  Keyframes or time              CConnectionAnimation.
  A list of what's in the world  CScene.


What goes where
---------------
  Scene                  Drawn as                     Today
  CMesh                  CRasterMesh                  Done: CRasterMesh::Build
  CInstance+CConnection  SRasterInstance              BoardScene writes axes and offsets by hand
  CCamera+CConnection    CRasterCamera                Viewers set CRasterCamera up directly
  CLight                 CRasteriser's light values   Fixed: msLight and RASTER_AMBIENT
  CConnectionAnimation   (changes CConnection)        Nothing.  BoardScene sets a yaw


Steps
-----
Each is done when something needs it, with tests.

1. Replace CFakeTracker in CScene and CInstance, so that an instance refers to its mesh and connections.
2. CConnection updates: a world matrix from a local one and a parent's.
3. The step from a scene to SRasterInstances: axes and offset from a connection's world matrix, in one scene-wide scale.
   Decide what happens to a mirrored matrix (mbParity), as SRasterInstance must turn and not mirror, and to one that scales.
4. The camera: CCamera and its connection set up a CRasterCamera.  ECameraType decides the projection, so ERasterProjection
   isn't set separately, and FOV becomes focal length.  Drag, Pan, Zoom and FitOrbit stay on CRasterCamera as viewer controls.
   Decide how they write back to the scene, if they should.
5. Lights: CRasteriser takes light values, in place of msLight and RASTER_AMBIENT, with what they are now as the default.
6. Move BoardScene onto a CScene: its models, the mainboard and boards standing in their sockets become instances and
   connections, and its yaw a connection or an animation.  This is what proves the steps above.
7. CConnectionAnimation: keyframes change connections, and the step in 3 runs again.

Not planned: skinning.  CInstance's maiConnections allows for it, but RasterLib has none, so it is new work of its own.
